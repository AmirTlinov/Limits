One current picture

The account, the pace, and the reset belong together.

Limits combines official account totals with local usage metadata. The window explains the trend; the menu bar keeps the next limit close; the widget receives only a small display snapshot.

  • CredentialsSaved in macOS Keychain and replaced through validated rollback-safe transactions.
  • HistoryStored locally in SQLite with 90-day per-turn detail and reproducible rate cards.
  • UpdatesThe current release gate requires Developer ID signing, Apple notarization, and an EdDSA-signed Sparkle feed.

Local by design

Your work labels stay on your Mac.

Limits has no developer analytics, advertising SDK, cloud account, or crash-reporting service. The public privacy contract names the exact rollout fields it extracts, where each record lives, and the few network requests the app makes.
